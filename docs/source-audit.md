The Scottish government page directly links the 2022 DZ/IZ metadata. CSW distribution records also expose the older editions and official NRS WFS services. Archived NRS pages are useful context, but public file/service routes are preferable where available. Frozen postcode polygon products with PSGA restrictions were excluded from public-download indexing.

The new ONS alpha catalogue was inspected for combined authorities. Its own licence note recommends the established portal until March 2027. An undated “Latest Combined Authority” service was therefore not used to backfill any historical year. The broad established-portal scan remains the automated inventory source.

For mayoral discovery, names/codes were read from full-resolution ONS CA service attributes with `returnGeometry=false` for 11 separate snapshots. The December 2025 and May 2025 files contain 15 authorities; the May 2024 file contains 11. CCA classification for East Midlands, Devon and Torbay, Greater Lincolnshire and Lancashire is retained with the official [devolution report](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/annual-report-on-english-devolution-2024-to-2025/secretary-of-states-annual-report-on-english-devolution-2024-25) source. Shared boundary variants at each audited vintage use those names/codes. The files also contain non-mayoral authorities; the index does not infer historical mayoral status. Newer 2026 authority polygons have not been found in the dated ONS series; four establishment instruments provide reference routes instead.

Centroid weighting is explicit metadata, separate from lookup fit methods and spatial-translation weights. ONS OA/LSOA/MSOA population and 2001 address methods are taken from labelled titles. The [Workplace Zone methodology](https://www.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/08efe60ec0934f6fbb49b9fc5537ae33/data) was read: the 2011 WZ point is weighted by Census worker counts with a Median Center algorithm, moved inside the boundary where necessary. It is classified as employment/workplace weighted with this provenance; it is not a BRES job-weighted point. Searching the official ONS organisation for employment/workplace-weighted wording found no additional separately named products. Curated centroid titles without explicit weighting remain unspecified.

## What the expansion does not certify

- Exhaustive annual legal boundary histories, every local electoral order, every geometry revision or every bespoke local reporting area.
- That absence from a source index means abolition, or that a census vintage remained valid for every later reporting date.
- Complete separate polygon series for CCAs, Scottish community councils or NI Assembly areas. Some official files/lookup routes cover these through shared or higher geographies. The relevant gaps remain visible.
- Census-to-census area/population weights across all systems. Indexed NRS linkage and Scottish matching products retain their publisher definitions; no weights are invented.
- That title-only ONS classification resolves the legal type of every constituent feature. LAD files intentionally group districts, UAs, metropolitan districts, London boroughs, Welsh authorities, council areas and LGDs.
- Automatic re-discovery of the curated non-ONS pages, or successful access to every linked GIS file. The landing-page audit is separate from file/service availability.

## Verification

The refresh follow-up adds a weekly/manual GitHub workflow with a review PR, a generated per-link report, a published-snapshot age warning, and flags for historical records no longer found in publisher discovery. Discovery, metadata capture and HTTP link checks have separate evidence. Missing, restricted and inconclusive responses remain distinct; a timeout is not evidence that a geography or file has been removed. Candidate validation prevents a broad failed scan or service outage from replacing the existing catalogue. Historical records stay searchable and are flagged instead of being deleted.

The 10 October live discovery scan returned the same 3,291 ONS items and 3,077 catalogue records as before, with no changed listing URLs. The refresh checks standalone download links and curated sources without downloading or mirroring GIS contents. It uses current ONS item discovery and ArcGIS/Hub definitions for service-backed records because an HTTP-200 portal shell does not establish dataset existence. Redirect query tokens are omitted from persisted reports. Curated sources and manually audited membership/methodology overrides still need occasional human review.

The workflow behaviour is documented in [GitHub's schedule and event documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/reference/workflows-and-actions/events-that-trigger-workflows), [built-in token documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/concepts/security/github_token), and the pinned [create-pull-request action](https://github.com/peter-evans/create-pull-request). Automatic update PRs require the repository's Actions PR-creation setting. Tests run before opening an update PR; GitHub can require approval to run separate CI for a token-created PR. Inactivity can disable schedules on a public repository, so the visible age warning and manual run route are retained.

The download/schema follow-up captured ArcGIS service and layer definitions plus Hub dataset metadata on 9 October 2026 (UTC). It enriches the existing 6 October ONS inventory rather than claiming a new search scan. The cache has 2,275 layer schemas across 2,274 source records, with 2,273 publisher-confirmed download records, 775 direct-link records and 29 unconfirmed records. No feature queries, generated exports or GIS downloads were made. Five curated OSNI services returned anonymous-access denials; the GLA schema was captured but its Hub download status was not found. WFS/CSW and standalone file schemas remain outside this automated enrichment.

The photographed [Combined Authorities December 2025 BFC layer](https://geoportal.statistics.gov.uk/datasets/46d178d0a81241a0ae8637b9eddf2378_1/about) is **layer 1**, not layer 0. Its [Hub metadata](https://hub.arcgis.com/api/v3/datasets/46d178d0a81241a0ae8637b9eddf2378_1) reports `downloadable: true`, 15 records and citation dates of 10 March 2026 (revision) and 24 April 2026 15:35:20 (publication). Its item metadata modification is 29 April 2026 08:24:10 UTC; item creation is 23 April 2026 18:14:26 UTC. The publisher citation timestamps have no offset, so the index does not invent one. Its [layer definition](https://services1.arcgis.com/ESMARspQHYMw9BZ9/arcgis/rest/services/Combined_Authorities_December_2025_Boundaries_EN_BFC/FeatureServer/1?f=pjson) exposes CAUTH25CD, CAUTH25NM, BNG_E, BNG_N, LONG, LAT and system fields, with CRS 27700. Schema capture does not certify field names for every exported format.

Date interpretation follows Esri's [content-date documentation](https://doc.esri.com/en/arcgis-hub/latest/build/manage-content-items.html): publisher metadata can override the item-created and update defaults. The index retains the date basis and separates item information updates from explicit revision dates or service `dataLastEditDate`. Download availability follows an explicit Hub flag or an indexed direct link; [service-supported formats](https://doc.esri.com/en/arcgis-hub/latest/build/data-download-settings.html) are displayed as capabilities rather than invented direct download links. Unknown status is not evidence that no download exists. A disabled flag would be shown only when all captured layers explicitly report downloads disabled.

Regression checks cover missing/null metadata, actual nonzero layer IDs, no feature/export requests, precise and unspecified-zone dates, publisher overrides, mixed-layer dates, capability-only uncertainty, disabled versus direct downloads, failed-refresh retention, stale-cache association and schema-bearing exports. Desktop/mobile browser checks exercised the photographed source, the downloadable filter, schema expansion and CSV/JSON downloads; downloaded exports were parsed to verify fields, dates and availability.

Regression checks cover upload-date/vintage separation, mixed-year lookups, unknown country scope, regional classification, national census baselines, acronym search, country containment, pre-2010 opt-in, CSV escaping and coverage-cell drill-down equality. Browser checks exercise representative filters and grid navigation. The source audit checks curated landing pages and representative download headers without mirroring polygons.

The follow-up adds checks for authority-name phrase matching across historical snapshots, CCA membership, GLA date handling, population/employment filter separation, documented workforce weighting, and weighted coverage-cell/filter equality. New GLA/methodology/legislation landing pages and PDF routes returned HTTP 200.

The boundary-detail follow-up preserves nine previously unclassified title-coded products: five BGG, three BGE and one BUE. Code labels use the official [ONS digital-boundary definitions](https://www.ons.gov.uk/methodology/geography/geographicalproducts/digitalboundaries) and [ONS geography training](https://onsgeo.github.io/geospatial-training/docs/practical_geog_and_stats). BGG is labelled “Generalised grid” without assuming a single grid size across vintages. The cached official Cancer Alliances April 2020 item explicitly describes BUE as 500m generalisation to the extent of the realm. BGE stays “See publisher specification”: the cached GEOSTAT 2011 item describes a grid extent product, while Urban Areas 2001 describes a generalised grid. No new polygon downloads or source refresh is implied by this metadata correction. Tests cover every indexed variant, coverage/filter agreement and empty-row partitioning with legacy, unlabelled and mixed-vintage files.
